Jobs recovery hardening evidence — 2026-09-27

Scope
- Audited `src/server/jobs.ts` lease recovery, bounded retry/dead-letter, unknown/no-resend behavior, idempotency and tenant-scoped access.
- No provider secrets, credentials, payload bodies or runtime configuration are included here.

Finding and fix
- Before this change, `recoverStaleJobs` selected `state='running' AND lease_until<=now()`.
- The deployed jobs schema permits a `running` row with a NULL lease (`lease_token` and `lease_until` are nullable). Such a row was never recovered and could remain stuck indefinitely.
- Minimal fix: recovery now selects `state='running' AND (lease_until IS NULL OR lease_until<=now())`.
- Recovery remains tenant-scoped by the caller's transaction-local RLS context. External stale work still transitions to `unknown`; local stale work transitions to `retry` or `dead` according to bounded attempts.
