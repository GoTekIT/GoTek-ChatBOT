Concrete fix
- Found defaultWorkspaceProviderInvoke() in src/server/worker.ts selected a chat grant
  using g.active but did not require the H28 expiry predicate. A grant could expire after
  AI job preparation and still pass the provider lookup, causing provider I/O before the
  later commit fence rejected the result. Added:
    (g.expires_at IS NULL OR g.expires_at>clock_timestamp())
  to the provider lookup. This prevents spend after grant expiry.
- Added tests/provider-grant-expiry.test.ts. It verifies an expired grant fails with
  AI_MODEL_REVOKED and invokes zero provider calls, then verifies an unexpired grant can
  reach the injected transport.

Verified contract slices
- Supported chat adapters are covered by deterministic wire-shape tests:
  Gemini, Anthropic, claude_code, OpenAI, ChatGPT and custom_llm.
- Embedding adapters and malformed vector rejection are covered for Gemini and
  OpenAI-compatible routes.
- Provider HTTP, timeout, network and malformed-response failures use stable redacted
  errors; response bodies and provider secrets are not returned.
- Provider redirects are disabled and custom endpoints are checked against HTTPS/private
  DNS policy in the tested paths.
- Usage metadata maps to the common receipt shape used by token metering.
- Expired/revoked/disabled model grants are rejected by the tested H28 policy and runtime
  boundary.

Acceptance limits
- Live provider receipts for Gemini/OpenAI/Anthropic/custom_llm are UNKNOWN / NEEDS
  VERIFICATION because tests use injected fetchers and no production/test provider key was
  supplied.
- Provider latency, rate-limit behavior, and vendor-specific streaming/tool-call schemas
  are not accepted by this deterministic contract suite.
- Full worker, browser, staging and production acceptance are outside this focused slice.
