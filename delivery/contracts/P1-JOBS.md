# D008 / H32.02 bounded jobs contract
Transactional PostgreSQL outbox/jobs, tenant-scoped with FORCE RLS. Enqueue inside business transaction; key unique per workspace, changed payload conflict. Worker receives explicit trusted tenant context, never client tenant. Claim SKIP LOCKED with lease token and finite attempts; stale worker cannot finish a replacement attempt. Expired lease for external side effect becomes unknown, NEVER auto replay. Safe local job may retry with exponential bounded backoff until dead. Confirm completion requires receipt ID. Explicit uncertain outcome becomes unknown; known failure-before-send can retry. External dispatch remains unimplemented/disabled; no claim of channel delivery.
GET /api/jobs exposes metadata only to Owner/Admin, no payload/lease/receipt body. /settings/jobs operational list is GoTek design (not HiChat observed parity), loading/error/empty/refresh. No client retry UNKNOWN control. Restore quarantines queued/running/retry/unknown jobs as cancelled and clears lease to prevent replay. Tests concurrency, stale lease, bounded attempts, unknown no replay, idempotency and RLS.
